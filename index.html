Hamara Mool Sthan: Mahinam (Mithila)

Hamara parivar Mithila ke pavitra sthan Mahinam se juda hai. Yahan ki sanskriti, sanskar aur prem hamare parivar ki sabse badi dharohar hai.

Pujya Purvajon ka Aashirwad

Pujya Markhande Jha ji aur hamare sabhi bujurgon ne parivar ko hamesha ekta aur aapas ke samman ki seekh di. Sabhi beton aur aane wali peedhiyon ka sthan ek samaan aadarneeya hai.

Pavitra Functions aur Parivarik Milan

Chhath Puja, Durga Puja aur Kojagari jaise functions par sabhi sadasya aapas mein judte hain aur aashirwad lete hain.
